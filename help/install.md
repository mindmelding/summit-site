title: Install Summit

---

Summit is free on the App Store for iPhone. You need iOS 17 or later.

::::steps
### Find Summit

Open the App Store on your iPhone and search for **Summit: Hill Walks**, or tap **Download on the App Store** at [mindmelding.dev/summit](https://mindmelding.dev/summit).

### Install it

Tap **Get**. There's no account to create.

### Allow location

When Summit asks, choose **Allow While Using App**. It needs your location to plan routes from where you stand and to record your walk. See [Privacy](/privacy).
::::

## Updates

The App Store keeps Summit up to date if automatic updates are on. Updates keep your walks and your peak.

:::note[Moving to a new iPhone]
Your walks are stored only on your phone and aren't backed up to a Summit account, because there isn't one. They move with an iCloud or Finder backup of your iPhone.
:::
