---
title: Settings
description: Change units and choose how Summit's paper looks by day and by night.
---

Open settings with the gear button at the top of the **Walk** or **Goal** tab.

## Paper

Choose how Summit looks:

- **Sun** switches to dark at your local sunset and back to light at sunrise.
- **Light** stays light.
- **Dark** stays dark.

## Units

- **Feet, miles**
- **Meters, kilometers**

This changes every screen, the walk cards and the widget. You can also switch units with the **ft / m** switch on the Plan tab.

## About

Everything Summit records stays on your phone. See [Privacy](/privacy).
