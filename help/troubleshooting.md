## Summit won't open

Make sure you have the latest version: open the App Store, tap your profile picture and pull down to refresh, then tap **Update** next to Summit. If it still won't open, restart your iPhone. Your walks and peak are kept through updates. See [Install Summit](/install).

## "Route planning isn't available on this build."

This build was made without a route service key, so the Plan tab can't find routes. Tracking, goals and widgets still work. Use **Start free walk** on the Walk tab, and update from the App Store when a newer version is out.

## "No walkable loops found here. Try another start point."

The route service couldn't make a loop from your start pin. This happens on private land, in the middle of a large park, or far from any mapped path. Move the pin onto a street or a trailhead and tap **Try again**.

## "Couldn't reach the route service."

Planning needs an internet connection. Check your signal and tap **Try again**. If it says the route service is taking too long, try again, or move the start pin somewhere with more streets nearby.

## The routes have far less climb than I asked for

Summit looks for loops up to about 15 miles. In flat areas, even the longest loop may not reach your target, so you'll see the closest it found. Try a smaller target, pick a start near a hill, or plan a [start-to-end walk](/start-to-end) up and down one steep street.

## My climb looks low

- Check the Walk screen for **No barometer on this phone; using GPS for elevation.** GPS alone only banks climbs of 6 m or more, so gentle rolling ground can count for less.
- Climb shorter than 3 m, like a small rise, doesn't count. See [How climb is counted](/how-climb-is-counted).

## Distance isn't going up

If the Walk screen says **Location is off for Summit**, tap **Turn it on in Settings** and choose **While Using the App**. If location is on, step outside and give GPS a moment. Readings that are too rough are skipped until they settle.

## The widget says "No peak yet"

Pick a peak on the **Goal** tab. The widget updates shortly after.

## I got an "Unfinished walk" card

Summit closed during a walk. Tap **Resume** to keep going, **Save as is** to bank it, or **Discard**. See [Track a walk](/track-a-walk).
