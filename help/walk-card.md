---
title: Share a walk card
description: Share a picture of your walk, save it to Photos, or add it to a Strava activity.
---

Every walk ends with a card: your climb, the path you took, and how far up your peak it moved you.

## Share it

On the finished walk, or on any walk in your **Log**:

- **Share walk card** opens the share sheet, so you can send it in Messages or post it anywhere.
- **Save to Photos** saves it to your photo library. The first time, your iPhone asks for permission to add photos.
- **Open Strava** jumps to the Strava app.

## Add it to Strava

Strava takes the picture, not the numbers. Save the card to Photos, open your activity in Strava, and add the card as a photo.

:::note
If **Save to Photos** doesn't work, Summit doesn't have permission. Turn it on under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Summit**.
:::
