title: Widgets and Live Activity
description: See your peak on the home screen and lock screen, and your walk while the phone is locked.
---

Summit's widget shows how far up your peak you are. During a walk, a Live Activity shows your climb on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

## Add the widget

1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen or lock screen, then tap **Edit** or **Customize**.
2. Tap **Add Widget** and choose **Summit**.
3. Pick a size. The widget is called **Your peak**.

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Home screen, small | The peak's name, its outline, the percent climbed and how far to go |
| Home screen, medium | The same, larger, plus your next milestone |
| Lock screen, round | A ring filling toward the top, with the percent inside |
| Lock screen, rectangular | The peak's outline, name, percent and how far to go |
| Lock screen, above the clock | The peak's name and percent in one line |

Until you pick a peak, the widget says **No peak yet**. Tap it to open the **Goal** tab.

## The Live Activity

When you start a walk, your climb appears on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island on iPhones that have one. It shows your climb, your target if you have one, and the walk time. The clock stops while the walk is auto-paused.

Tap it to open the **Walk** tab. It goes away when you end the walk.

:::note
The widget uses the units you chose in Summit's settings. If you switch between feet and meters, the widget follows the next time it updates.
:::
