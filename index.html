How it works

Plan

Tell it how much climb you want, 200 to 1,000 ft. It finds loops from your front door that have it, and shows you the hill before you commit.

Walk

The barometer counts every foot you gain, on the route or off it. The plan is drawn in pencil; your walk inks over it as you go.

Goal

Your peak on a page: how high you've climbed, what's next on the route up, and the day it's stamped Summited.

Every walk ends with a card.

The number, the trail you actually took, and how far up the mountain it moved you. Save it to Photos and post it to Strava, or send it to whoever you're walking against.
