How it works

↓
  1. 01

    Ask for a climb

    Pick anywhere from 100 to 3,000 ft. Summit scouts the hills around you and hands back real walking loops that have it, with the elevation profile on each, so you see the hill before you commit.

  2. 02

    Walk it

    The barometer counts every foot you gain, on the route or off it. Your progress sits on the lock screen, and the walk keeps recording with the screen off or the phone in airplane mode.

  3. 03

    Bank it on a mountain

    Every walk's climb goes toward your peak: how high you are, the next landmark on the real route up, and the day the page is stamped Summited.

What it won't do

↓

It doesn't need an account

No sign-up, no login, no Summit server. Open it and walk.

It doesn't send your walks anywhere

Your paths, location and peaks stay on your iPhone. Planning sends only your start and end points to the route service. Anonymous usage and crash reports help us fix bugs and never include where you walked. Read the privacy policy.

It doesn't show ads or sell data

Summit is free, with no ads, and it never sells your data.

It doesn't inflate your climb

GPS noise is smoothed out and small wobbles don't count, so a flat walk stays flat. How climb is counted.
